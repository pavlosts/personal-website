Defaults are the real design

By Pavlos · Nov 2026
12

Most people never open settings. Whatever ships as the default becomes the product, for almost everyone, for the whole life of the feature.

So I've started designing defaults first and options last. It changes which questions get asked in review, and it makes the product feel opinionated in a calm way.
