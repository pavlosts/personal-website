Personal site: 9 directions

Each one is a full homepage mock with the same content, so you can compare the feeling rather than the copy. Click any to open it full size.

01 · Paper

Editorial, serif, warm

A quiet single column on cream paper. Reads like a well set book. The safest, most timeless option.

02 · Terminal

Mono, dark, keyboard first

Posts as files, keyboard navigation with j and k. Shows the tech side without feeling like a hacker cliché.

03 · Swiss

Grid, huge type, one red

European typographic tradition. Confident and structured, the most "designer" of the set.

04 · Index

Archive table, filters

Everything in one sortable list with working filters. Great once you have lots of posts, and it rewards consistency.

05 · Garden

Notes that grow, sage tones

A digital garden where notes are seedlings, budding, or evergreen. Removes the pressure to publish finished essays.

06 · Ambient

Night, soft glow, live clock

Calm dark mode with drifting light and a "currently" strip. Chill and introverted, with a modern edge.

07 · Bento

Tiles, playful, personal

A grid of small windows into your life: latest post, music, time, tools. The most personality per pixel.

08 · Letter

Intimate, signed, margin notes

The homepage is a short letter to the reader, with posts linked inline. Warm and human, built around a newsletter.

09 · Desk

Canvas, draggable, spatial

A dotted canvas like your Figma desk. Drag notes around. The most experimental and memorable, with a list view fallback.

Round 2: physical objects

Following the Desk direction. Each homepage is a real object you can touch: things move, flip, open, and get pulled out the way they would on a real table.

10 · Workbench

Wood table, top down

Your real desk from above: spiral notebook, index cards, coffee, pencil, phone playing music. Everything is draggable.

11 · Corkboard

Pins, string, tickets

Notes pinned to cork with red string linking related ideas. Hover straightens a note. Feels like thinking in progress.

12 · Bookshelf

Spines, shelves, open book

Each post is a book. One shelf per type. Click a spine and it opens as a two page spread to read.

13 · Card catalog

Library drawers, typed cards

Old library catalog. Drawers filter by type, cards flip with arrow keys and pull up to read. The most systematic of the set.

14 · Notebook

Dot grid, tabs, page turn

An open notebook with contents, colored tabs, an elastic band, and handwritten margin notes. Tabs turn the page.

15 · Postcards

Stamps, postmarks, flip

Every post is a postcard to the reader. Click to flip to the picture, drag to rearrange. Pairs naturally with a newsletter.

Round 3: timelines that branch

You land on the latest activity and follow a path back in time. Every interest is its own branch, colored by type: product design, writing, hobbies, new interests. Branches that ended stop growing. Live ones keep growing at the tip.

16 · Branches

Vertical, git style, filters

A clean history graph with one lane per branch. Growing tips pulse at the top, ended lanes cap off and turn grey. Hover a row to trace its branch.

17 · Tree

Fine line, draws in as you scroll

A living tree drawn in thin ink. Today is the canopy, 2019 is the roots. Live branches glow at the tip and carry leaves. Ended ones fade to grey and stop.

18 · Trail map

Paper map, horizontal, drag

A hiking map with contour lines. You are here at today, walk right into the past. Trails fork off the main route and end at a sign when I stopped.
