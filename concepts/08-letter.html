Hello, and thanks for stopping by.

I'm Pavlos, a product designer. I spend most of my days making software feel a little calmer and a little more obvious. It's quiet work, and I like it that way.

This site is where I think out loud. Lately I've been writing about why defaults matter more than features, and about software that waits for you instead of asking for attention.Both started as notes on my phone during walks. Most things here do.

If you're new, the notes on naming things are a good place to start. If you're a designer working mostly alone, this one is for you.

I write a short letter like this once a month. No tracking, no tricks, and you can leave any time.

Warmly,

Pavlos

P.S. I'm currently building a small tool for keeping design decisions in one place. If that sounds useful, reply and tell me why.
