The roots: four directions

The bottom of the tree, where you land after scrolling back to 2019. Each option keeps the fine-line style and gives the ending a job: summarising, orienting, or sending you back.

A

Root system and origin card

Fine hair roots that fade into the ground, and a glass card that summarises the whole tree.

B

Growth rings

The trunk meets the ground as a cross-section. One ring per year, oldest in the middle, today on the outside.

C

Roots that mirror the branches

One root per branch, in its colour, long for long branches. The bottom becomes a quiet index of everything.

D

The beginning, with a way back

The trunk ends in a single point on a ground line. Big type closes the story and a button takes you back to today.
